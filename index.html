01 — About

I'm a fourth-year electrical engineering student at UCLA working on RF and mmWave electronics. My interests span RF/mmWave circuit design, power electronics, and high-frequency systems.

Most of my experience is in test and measurement: characterizing power amplifiers, LNAs, mixers, and complete systems. I also design and lay out circuits using Keysight ADS, LTspice, and Altium Designer.
